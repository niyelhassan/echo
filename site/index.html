Captions that catch the laugh, too.

Live captions for any Chrome tab, with the sounds and tone around the words. Made on your Mac, for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers.

Install Tonal See how it works

Free to use. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac and Chrome 116 or later. Audio is processed on your Mac and never uploaded.

How it works

There’s no upload, cloud key or per-site setup. You start captions on the tab that’s playing.

  1. Start captions on a tab

    Open any video in Chrome and press Alt Shift C, or click the extension and choose Start captions.

  2. Audio goes to a local service

    Chrome passes the tab’s audio to a small program running on your Mac. You still hear the video, and your microphone isn’t used.

  3. Captions appear over the video

    They work in fullscreen and can be dragged anywhere. Alt Shift T opens the full transcript.

What appears on screen

Besides the words, Tonal marks laughter, music and other sounds, and sometimes how a voice sounded.

Speech
Transcribed by NVIDIA Parakeet on Apple’s MLX. Lines update every 0.4 s and settle after a short pause.
Sound cues
21 sounds, including laughter, applause, music, crying, sirens and doorbells, checked once a second.
Vocal tone
Shown only when one tone clearly outscores the rest. Use it as a hint: it’s often wrong and can’t detect sarcasm.
Transcript
Timestamped and searchable. You can copy it or save it as a text file.

Tested on real video

Tonal was measured against human-made captions on these four kinds of video.

  • Stand-up

    On videos it wasn’t tuned on, Tonal marked 6 of the 9 laughs that human captioners marked.

  • Talks and lectures

    Long sentences are split at the quietest moment, so captions keep up with a speaker who rarely pauses.

  • Film scenes

    The speech detector is tuned to pick up shouted lines over a music score. Tone tags only appear when the model is confident.

  • Sitcoms

    Quick exchanges are captioned line by line. Tonal doesn’t say who is speaking yet, so cuts between people can be hard to follow.

Privacy

Every model runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and there’s no cloud service or account.

  • Local models

    Parakeet, CED and emotion2vec run on Apple Silicon. After a one-time download, captioning works offline.

  • One allowed address

    The extension’s security policy only permits ws://127.0.0.1:8765. You can check this in manifest.json.

  • Nothing saved to disk

    Audio is kept in memory while it’s processed, then discarded.

  • No microphone access

    Tonal captures the tab you choose. Your mic is never used.

  • Paired connection

    The local service only responds to the extension it’s paired with, using a token only your user account can read.

  • Transcripts stay in the tab

    Nothing leaves the page unless you copy or save it.

Questions

How accurate are the captions?
Across TED talks, stand-up, a film scene and a sitcom sketch, Tonal got 10.6% of words wrong compared with human captions. Whisper large-v3-turbo scored 9.2% but took about 7 times as long, so Tonal uses Parakeet. These were measured on 7 November 2026 on an M3 Pro, and the scripts are in the repo.
Can I trust the tone labels?
Use them as a hint. They describe how a voice sounds to a model, not what someone feels. The thresholds were set by hand, the labels are often wrong, and sarcasm isn’t detected. You can make them stricter or turn them off.
What do I need?
An Apple Silicon Mac and Chrome 116 or later, with English audio. There’s no Windows or Linux version, and you can caption one tab at a time.
Can I change how captions look?
Yes. There are four text sizes, three color schemes (white or yellow on black, or black on white), a background from 60% to solid, two to four lines, and top or bottom placement. Settings sync with Chrome.
Does it work in fullscreen?
On ordinary web players, yes. Picture-in-picture, DRM-protected video and some embedded players haven’t been tested.

Install

  1. Clone the repository and run the installer. It downloads about 3 GB of models the first time.

    git clone https://github.com/niyelhassan/echo.git
cd echo
uv sync && uv run python install.py

  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and select the extension folder.

  3. On a tab with audio, press Alt Shift C.

Setup notes and test scripts are in the GitHub repository.
